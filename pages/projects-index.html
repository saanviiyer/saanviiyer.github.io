selected work

Research in three lanes: evaluations that survive optimizers, world models for biology and the brain, and clinical machine learning evaluated honestly. The question across all of it is whether a measurement still means what it claims once a model is used to choose, design, or diagnose. Each entry links to its full report.

evaluations that survive optimizers

Evaluations that survive optimizersNeurIPS 2026 workshops · 2026 Six evaluations testing whether a score keeps its meaning under pressure: fitness proxies, benchmark-to-selection, representational identifiability, verifier red-teaming, genomic-to-protein transfer, and a safety benchmark that leaks. PRISMoral, IEEE CIBCB 2026 · 2026 Every cancer mutational signature applied to proteins, and the controls that decide whether a repeatable embedding movement is biology or an artifact. AI evals and agents in life sciences3 NeurIPS 2026 posters · 2026 One question at three heights: a verifier that certifies a scorer which memorized the answers, a representation that cannot tell distinct biology apart, and a benchmark metric that stops predicting the decision made from it. Proposalsno results, by design · 2026 Two evaluations i want to run: a white-box probe of what a protein model's representation carries, and a portable audit that stress-tests any score under search.
