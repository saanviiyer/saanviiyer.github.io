Misc.

Music

Music is the thing I keep coming back to outside of research. I'm an amateur singer and songwriter, and I produce my own tracks in GarageBand, layering vocals and instrument parts to hear an idea turn into a finished song.

Most of my singing has been in choir. I sang lead alto in my school's top jazz choir, one of about twelve singers in the highest-level group, and I was a section leader in both the jazz and women's choirs. I was selected three times as a Washington All-State and All-Northwest performer, singing in the Treble, Symphonic, and Mixed choirs.

Beyond the school groups, I performed at state jazz festivals like Jazz for Thought and Northlake Jazz. I also sang as a volunteer at a local middle school, at senior care centers, and at the Bothell holiday market festival.

Things I've built

A handful of small apps and prototypes I've built on the side, mostly weekend-scale MVPs. Most run on mock data with no setup.

  • rxiver. An AI research workspace that recommends arXiv papers and lets you organize them into folders and think through them in named chat windows.
  • Deadline Radar. A dashboard of ML, neuroscience, and computational-biology conference deadlines, color-coded by urgency, with calendar export.
  • AuthzSwarm. A multi-agent harness that runs authorized defensive security checks against apps you own and reports the bugs it finds. Detect-and-report, not exploit.
  • DraftDeck. Drafts social posts with AI, where nothing publishes until you approve each one.
  • LifeCoach AI. Turns a goal into a phased roadmap with daily objectives, plus shared rooms where an AI coach joins the chat.
  • Resume Tailor. Reviews a resume against a job posting and drafts a tailored version and application answers, without fabricating anything.
  • ConcertMatch. Reads your top artists and genres and matches them to real concerts near you.
  • mixcut. Shortens a song for DJ mixes by cutting a section and splicing the ends with a beat-aligned crossfade.
  • handsynth. Play synth chords with your hands using webcam hand-tracking, entirely in the browser.
  • JobTinder. A swipe-to-match interface for candidates and jobs.
  • Girl Math. A daily-budget tracker where the money you don't spend rolls over into a surplus.
  • thank you. A feed-first social app for sharing everyday kindness and sending thanks, built for web and native iOS.
