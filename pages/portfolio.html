selected projects

PRISM applies all 78 COSMIC single-base-substitution signatures to protein-coding sequences, embeds the wild-type and mutant proteins with ESM-2, and measures how far each signature moves the mutants toward Pfam protein families, with controls that decide whether the movement is biological. SBS17a ranks first of 78 toward PF17041 across two independent clans, yet a composition-matched control reproduces 72% of that movement and protein length explains 92.5% of the displacement, so it reads as an embedding effect rather than a validated functional driver. oral, IEEE CIBCB 2026, accepted for additional venues [TODO: Saanvi to name the additional venues]. code, full page.

CortSeer adjusts a 3D nnU-Net detection threshold by anatomical zone and runs a lobe-restricted multi-peak search to recover epileptogenic lesions a detector would otherwise miss. as an upper bound under oracle localization, the lobe-restricted search recovered 14 of 24 missed lesions, which is 58%, against 1.4 expected by chance, with P below 0.001. oral, ECCV AI for Medical 3D Vision workshop 2026. [TODO: Saanvi to confirm the Brain Informatics submission before it is claimed.] workshop, full page.

gauntlet is an audit battery that stress-tests a protein fitness proxy against trivial baselines and reward hacking before anyone ranks designs with it. on 504 measured PET-hydrolase variants, an ESM-2 650M zero-shot score ranks activity at a Spearman of 0.01 while a plain mutation count reaches 0.25. poster, AI for Science at NeurIPS 2026. code, full page.

faithful heading trains action-conditioned world models on a robot's range sensors with no pose supervision and measures how closely a ring in the hidden state follows true heading deep inside a sensory blackout. a blackout-trained model tracks heading at a fidelity of 0.99 against 0.58 for an always-on model, and the variable behaves as a causally steerable continuous attractor. lightning talk and poster, RoBoWoMo at IEEE IROS 2026. more research

most of my research is one question: do model evaluations survive contact with an optimizer? i build the baselines, controls, and stress tests first, then check whether a metric measures what it claims or just something easy to game.

biologos matches a genomic model and a protein model on the same 25 deep mutational scanning assays to ask whether a DNA-trained model knows protein function or only looks like it does. a genomic model at Evo 2 7B scale reaches a mean Spearman of 0.266, statistically indistinguishable from a BLOSUM62 substitution matrix at 0.228 and far below ESM-2 650M at 0.466. paper, code, blog.

action identifiability tests whether a protein-design agent's apparent success identifies the biological action behind it, by checking whether distinct mutational processes stay distinguishable in a model's representation across all 3,003 pairs of 78 processes. distinct processes often reach the same destination, and a translation placeholder count explains up to an R-squared of 0.870 of pairwise destination distance in ESM-2 650M. poster, AgenticLS at NeurIPS 2026, and presented at MoML 2026, MIT. paper.

benchmark integrity scores a hazardous-knowledge safety benchmark with rules that never read the question, to see how much of the score is an answer-writing artifact. a longest-answer rule scores 46.5% and a question-blind classifier 34.6% against 25% chance, so part of the score reflects how the options were written rather than knowledge. blog post. blog.

engram asks whether biologically inspired local learning rules forget less than backpropagation once both are compared at the same learning accuracy on class-incremental Split-CIFAR-10. the best local rule retains 0.21 points more, with a 95% interval of [+0.11, +0.31], while replay adds 10.6, so locality itself does not reduce forgetting on this protocol. poster, AI4MetaScience at NeurIPS 2026. blog, code to be released. crosstalk builds reinforcement-learning environments for protein binding specificity and measures how specificity prediction changes with model scale. binding specificity does not improve, and becomes harder to capture, as models get larger. [TODO: Saanvi to add the exact effect size.] code. code.

plimsoll is a tool for researchers auditing their own work: it checks a repository against a methodology checklist, looking for trivial baselines, verified nulls, decisive controls, named confounds, and printed denominators, and it quotes the evidence behind each verdict. it is built to catch an overstated number before it ships, not to make a research claim of its own. code. code.

white-box evaluation is a proposal. the question is whether a protein model has actually learned an assay property or is shortcutting it, and the proposed method ranks already-measured benign variants, trains linear probes on the model's internal activations, and holds whole protein families out at test time. it would be falsified if the probes recovered the property just as well on held-out families as on seen ones for a model whose output score is known to shortcut, and it reports no results. proposal. proposal.
