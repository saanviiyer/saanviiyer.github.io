An interactive cryptography lab that teaches RSA, Diffie-Hellman, ElGamal, elliptic curves, AES-128, SHA-256 and digital signatures step by step. Every number is computed live in the browser with math written from scratch, and an attacks corner breaks badly built RSA with small exponents, shared moduli and weak primes. It is a teaching tool, not a library to use.
