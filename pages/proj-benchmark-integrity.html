benchmark integrity

An audit of what a safety benchmark actually measures. On WMDP-Bio, a rule that never reads the question and picks the longest of four options scores 46.5% against 25% chance, and a question-blind classifier reaches 34.6%, so part of the score is an artifact in how the answer options were written rather than biological knowledge.

The write-up reports aggregate findings only, with no item text and no recipe for exploiting the artifact.
