Make the model roar

A transformer has a spine that carries everything forward, ribs that reach back for what came before, and a skull where the answer leaves. So I drew one as a jaguar. Hover a bone to see what that part computes, take a bone out, then make it roar and hear what is left.

How to read this. I set every weight by hand so each bone does one job you can watch, and I did not train the model. The ribs are the attention heads. The long ribs reach one word back and copy it onto the spine, and the short ribs stay on the word the jaguar is standing on. The spine is the residual stream, which carries what every part wrote. The chest is the feed-forward layer, which amplifies whatever the spine carries most loudly. The legs are the normalisation steps that keep the numbers level. The answer leaves through the skull. Take out the long ribs and the jaguar still roars, but it roars the word under its feet.
