personal projects

things i have built to chase a question, with a short note on why.

Adversarial evaluations on proteins

gauntlet is an audit battery that stress-tests protein fitness proxies for trivial baselines and reward hacking. A plain mutation count beat ESM-2 zero-shot on measured PETase activity. shibboleth points the same battery at biosecurity screening, where proxies collapse at real operating points, and crosstalk tests binding specificity, which gets worse as models get bigger. plimsoll is the same instinct as a tool. It holds any research repo against a methodology checklist before it ships a number. The shared question is whether a proxy has earned the decision it is used for.

ML for protein engineering

PRISM applies all 78 cancer mutational signatures to protein-coding sequences and measures how each one moves ESM-2 embeddings across protein families. genmodels asks whether generative models have implicitly learned those evolutionary biases. This was supervised independent research with AITHYRA, and it is where my audit discipline came from. The pipeline ended up auditing its own metric.

Predictive world models

faithful heading finds that predictive world models trained from pixels alone recover a faithful heading variable with no pose supervision, but only under partial observability. I ran ML simulations to understand how a robot perceives its environment from pixels alone, which connects to my interest in how brains build internal models of space from partial observations. Lightning talk and poster at the RoBoWoMo workshop, IEEE IROS 2026.

Biologically inspired learning

engram is a controlled test of whether biologically inspired learning rules improve retention once training budget is matched. A fancier rule should beat a fair baseline, not a weak one.

For fun

I build small tools and toys outside research.

research tools

  • lattice is a research workspace for annotating PDFs and taking Notion-style linked notes over a knowledge graph of your papers.
  • rxiver is an arXiv workspace that recommends papers, sorts them into folders, and lets you chat with them.
  • paperclip is a cleaner Zotero for saving papers, grouping them by topic, and running AI analysis across a collection.
  • gloss lets you highlight a sentence on a page or PDF and read an LLM explanation in a tooltip.
  • Deadline Radar tracks ML, neuroscience, and computational-biology conference deadlines and exports them to your calendar.

music and creative

  • mixcut auto-cuts a song's second verse and chorus into a beat-aligned splice to shorten it for mixes.
  • handsynth plays synth chords from webcam hand tracking in the browser.
  • trackstar plays a synth with hand gestures, then produces multitrack mixes with AI beats.
  • concertmatch turns your music taste into concert recommendations with tickets nearby.
  • deejai is a set of command-line music-production tools for balancing vocal takes and building beats.
  • dice turns hand gestures into music controls in the browser and on desktop.
  • concert-scout ranks concert tickets from your listening taste using official event APIs.

apps and experiments

  • overheard is a language-practice app with spoken AI conversations and review scoring.
  • resume-tailor turns a job posting into a tailored resume and drafted application answers.
  • lifecoach-ai turns your goals into a roadmap with daily objectives and shared team chat rooms.
  • jobtinder is Tinder for recruiting, where you swipe to match candidates and jobs.
  • girl-math sets a daily budget and rolls unspent money into a growing surplus.
  • draftdeck drafts your social posts and publishes nothing until you approve each one.

  • clipwright turns longform film into 9:16 vertical shorts with burned-in captions.

  • videogist turns a YouTube link or lecture series into a summary, a timestamped outline, and takeaways.
  • cellbio-shorts renders narrated vertical explainer videos on cell biology and physics.
  • science-instagram-bot drafts captioned science posts for Instagram.
  • hackillini26 is my HackIllinois 2026 Mechathon web app.
  • thank you is a feed-first social app for sharing everyday kindness, with web and iOS clients.
  • hunt-the-wumpus is a playful browser take on the classic Hunt the Wumpus game.

  • neural-steering-lab is a front-end concept page for an embodied interpretability workbench.
