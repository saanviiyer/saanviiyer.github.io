  • science olympiad. varsity captain, 30+ medals across regional, state and national competitions, including 1st nationally in Detector Building at the 2024 MIT Invitational.
  • singing. lead alto in my school's jazz choir and a 3x Washington All-State and All-Northwest performer (music resume).
