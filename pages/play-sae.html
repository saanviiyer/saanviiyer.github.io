Sparse autoencoders, live

A sparse autoencoder pulls apart the tangled features a model packs into too few dimensions. I train them from scratch in your browser on a toy model where I planted the true features, so you can watch every step happen. The ten chapters follow two guides, Adam Karvonen's intuitive explanation and a gentle introduction on LessWrong, and each chapter ends with their key points. Your browser computes everything, and I precomputed nothing.
