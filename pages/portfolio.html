experiences

ai for radiologists

Stanford Pediatric Neuroradiology Lab, AIMI
  • Built AI brain lesion detection software application, in trial with clinical radiologists at Stanford.
  • Post-training and evals for clinical EEG foundation models.
  • Awarded Caltech SURF.
Stanford Pediatric Neuroradiology Lab, AIMI

It was great collaborating with radiologists on this project, and building software for the division. My goal was to build lesion detection for epilepsy using MRI and EEG information, simulating the multimodal nature of a clinician's workflow, and this work is in publication. This experience was especially fun as my first summer in SF, where I grew a lot and got to meet exceptional teams building at the frontier of AI x Bio.

Caltech Computer Vision Lab
  • Built a CT image encoder for kidney tumors (pretraining and fine-tuning).
  • Collaborated to build a CLIP-style model that links 3D CT scans with tumor gene mutations.
Caltech Computer Vision Lab

This was my first research experience at Caltech, and first time working with CLIP. I learned about foundation models, encoders and decoders, and was supervised by Raphaela Kang and Joe Rich. This project is still ongoing!

projects

gauntlet stress-tests protein fitness proxies for trivial baselines and reward hacking, and a plain mutation count beat ESM-2 zero-shot on measured PETase activity. code, and a poster at WiML, NeurIPS 2026.

shibboleth points the same battery at biosecurity screening, where the proxies collapse at real operating points. code

crosstalk measures binding specificity, which gets worse as models get bigger. code

plimsoll holds any research repo against a methodology checklist before it ships a number. code

PRISM applies all 78 cancer mutational signatures to protein sequences and measures how each one moves ESM-2 embeddings across protein families. code, and an oral at IEEE CIBCB 2026.

genmodels asks whether generative models implicitly learned those evolutionary biases. code

biologos measures whether genomic-to-protein transfer survives its controls, and finds a genomic language model's protein-fitness signal indistinguishable from a substitution matrix. code

faithful heading shows predictive world models trained from pixels alone recover a faithful heading variable only under partial observability. A lightning talk and poster at the RoBoWoMo workshop, IEEE IROS 2026.

engram is a controlled test of whether biologically inspired learning rules improve retention once training budget is matched. code

CortSeer maps EEG-identified seizure onset zones to anatomy on a multimodal MRI and EEG foundation model, and is submitted to Brain Informatics. An oral at the ECCV AI for Medical 3D Vision workshop.

Also presenting at CBEC (Chan Zuckerberg Biohub), the Lake Conference, ISCB Great Plains, Salk Cancer Day, and the 2026 Cascadia Mucosal Biology Symposium.

outreach

Bothell SWENext. I founded my high school's SWENext club, part of the Society of Women Engineers' pre-college program. The club grew to over 100 members in its first year, running hackathons, guest speaker events, and workshops for outreach, mentorship, and career exploration, with a focus on marginalized identities in computing. SWE recognized it with a 2023 WE Local Award, and NCWIT named me a 2024 Aspirations in Computing Impact Award recipient for the work.
