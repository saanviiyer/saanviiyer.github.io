faithful heading

Predictive world models trained from pixels alone recover a faithful heading variable with no pose supervision, but only under partial observability. I ran ML simulations to understand how a robot perceives its environment from pixels alone, which connects to my interest in how brains build internal models of space from partial observations. Lightning talk and poster at the RoBoWoMo workshop, IEEE IROS 2026.
