Integrated Core RFP

ML-Guided Directed Evolution to Engineer Thermostable and Alkaliphilic PETases.

I wrote this proposal in the spring term of my freshman year, and it is where I first got interested in the power of proteins. It sets out a machine-learning-guided directed evolution plan to engineer PETases, the enzymes that break down PET plastic, so that they stay active at high temperature and in alkaline conditions where industrial plastic processing happens.
