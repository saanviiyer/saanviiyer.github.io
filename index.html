Studying intelligence in living systems

Saanvi S. Subramanian

Undergraduate at Caltech, studying Computation and Neural Systems with a minor in Math and Information Science, advised by Markus Meister.

I use machine learning to study how brains and other living systems work. I am drawn to questions that can lead to useful interventions. A change to a neural circuit or biological pathway can directly change a patient's condition, which gives the research a clear connection to health.

I am especially curious about neuro-based world models: how brains build internal representations of the world and use them to guide behavior. I am beginning to explore how these ideas relate to AI alignment. AI's growing ability to reason about biology also raises biosecurity questions about how new capabilities should be evaluated and controlled.
