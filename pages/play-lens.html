Neuron lens, in miniature

Neuron Lens lets you walk through the neurons of an 8-billion-parameter model and read what each one does. Your browser cannot hold a model that size, so this page trains a small one on a language I generated, then writes every description from that neuron's own weights and activations.

Training the model in your browser.

The neurons appear once training finishes.

What the labels mean. A neuron fires when its activation rises above zero, and I call the words that raise it the most its trigger words. The predicts and suppresses lists come straight from the neuron's output weights, which add to or subtract from each word's score when the neuron fires. No language model writes these descriptions, so they say only what the weights say, and a neuron that does several jobs at once will look vague here. That is the honest version of what interpretability at this scale can tell you.
