PRISM

PRISM applies all 78 cancer mutational signatures to protein-coding sequences and measures how each one moves ESM-2 embeddings across protein families. It was supervised independent research with AITHYRA, and it is where my audit discipline came from. The pipeline ended up auditing its own metric.
