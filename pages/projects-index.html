selected work

Research in three lanes: evaluations that survive optimizers, world models for biology and the brain, and clinical machine learning evaluated honestly. The question across all of it is whether a measurement still means what it claims once a model is used to choose, design, or diagnose. Each entry links to its full report.

evaluations that survive optimizers

Action identifiability in protein-design agentsAgenticLS @ NeurIPS · MoML 2026 whether the representation a design agent acts through can tell its candidate actions apart: distinct mutational processes collapse to the same ESM-2 destination, and the separation that remains is substitution composition, not biology. When the verifier is wrongVerify-Agents @ NeurIPS 2026 red-teaming the trust gate of a closed-loop design agent: an in-sample gate certifies a scorer that memorized the answers at AUROC 0.991, and catching the attack is not recovering its cost. Does benchmark correlation predict selection quality?Trust-AI-Eval · GEM-Bio @ NeurIPS 2026 reported benchmark correlation explains half the variance in top-10% selection value and 16% at top-1%, and the same number means different things across benchmark families. Gauntlet: stress-testing protein fitness proxiesAI for Science @ NeurIPS 2026 a zero-shot protein score ranks measured PETase activity at 0.01, below a plain mutation count at 0.25, so it adds nothing over counting on the task it is used for. PRISMoral, IEEE CIBCB 2026 every cancer mutational signature applied to proteins, and the controls that decide whether a repeatable embedding movement is biology or an artifact of how the question was asked. Proposalsno results, by design two evaluations i want to run: a white-box probe of what a protein model's representation carries, and a portable audit that stress-tests any score under search.

world models for biology and the brain

Which latent coordinates drive world-model dynamics?Robotics World Modeling @ NeurIPS 2026 a probe can read a coordinate a world model's dynamics never use, and which coordinates it builds is set by what the observation makes separable, not the actuator count. A steerable heading variable, and continual learningRoBoWoMo @ IEEE IROS 2026 when a predictive model builds a usable internal variable and when continued training erases one: a steerable heading variable that emerges only under partial observability, and continual learning where a fair baseline erases the advantage of local rules.
