white-box evaluation

A white-box evaluation for protein models. It ranks fixed sets of already-measured benign variants, recovers the assay property with linear probes on a model's internal activations, and holds out whole families to check that the signal transfers rather than shortcutting.

The biosecurity boundary is fixed first: only benign, already-measured proteins, no new sequences, and no reusable steering probe.
