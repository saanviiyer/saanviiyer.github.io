About Me

✦   Hello! I'm Saanvi, an undergraduate at Caltech studying Computation and Neural Systems with a minor in Information Science and Math, where I'm advised by Markus Meister.

I build technologies to understand the brain — working at the intersection of neuroscience, AI, and computational biology. My research spans multimodal foundation models for medical imaging, noninvasive brain stimulation, and computational modeling of cancer and neural development.

Outside the lab, I lead Caltech's Neurotechers and Neuro/ML groups, sing, and spend as much time as I can outdoors.
