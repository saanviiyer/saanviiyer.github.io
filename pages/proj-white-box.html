An output-level evaluation is gameable and incomplete, because the output is a projection of what the model actually did. The complement is reading the computation. This project does that inside protein and nucleotide language models, and most of the work turns out to be building an instrument you can trust.

The biosecurity boundary is fixed first and does not move: only benign, already-measured proteins, no new sequences generated, no wet-lab validation, and no reusable steering probe released.

Three questions

Where does functional information become causally accessible inside ESM-2 — at what depth does intervening along a fitted functional direction move the model's own output representation? How is it organised there — is the sparse dictionary element attribution selects the element that carries the effect? And for nucleotide models, do the tests we would use to answer the first two discriminate at all?

Each of them was mine.

Update, 18 October 2026: the wider replication

I re-ran the depth sweep on an A100 with roughly double the held-out panel — 22 proteins, 11 train and 11 test, all 15 layers — and separately ran an intervention with no dose parameter at all. Both weakened the claim they were meant to sharpen.

The number replicates; its location does not. Restricted to the nine held-out proteins under 260 residues, layer 24 gives +0.0929 against the +0.0933 I had reported. Same number. But its interval is [−0.1393, +0.3922], and of the eight layers whose intervals exclude zero, none is in the band. Only two of eleven test proteins are longer than 260 residues and the short-only subset behaves the same way, so this is what eleven proteins can resolve, not an artifact of admitting longer proteins.

And the margin is confounded with injection depth, at a rank correlation of −0.873 against layers-remaining-to-readout. A perturbation injected one layer from the output barely has to survive any computation. Under the dose-free intervention, with every null held to the same edit magnitude, every band layer has a negative margin — the generic mean shift slightly beats the fitted direction there — and the only layers that clear zero are at depths 0.48 and 0.61.

† Two faults in my own new script surfaced from its own output first: the nulls were not edit-matched (the stiff null was editing 2.9× harder, and its extra damage was reading as the fitted direction's specificity), and one mode collapsed the within-sequence spread along the direction from 1.087 to exactly zero. Both are fixed and both are now assertions in its self-test.

A separate claim I had written up two days earlier — that a label-free intrinsic-dimension heuristic picks the causally accessible layer, at a correlation of −0.783 — does not survive either. On the wider panel it is −0.310 across all layers and −0.055 in the interior. It was the depth confound I had already named, measured on too few layers to separate from it. Withdrawn.

So the defensible version is narrower than what is stated above: a fitted direction beats a generic shift somewhere in the interior, the effect is small, and it resolves nearer half depth than three-quarter depth. I have left the original claim on this page rather than quietly editing it, because a claim that moves under its own better-powered replication should be shown moving.

The instrument is the hard part

Three results drove that home. A reading-frame test I built is passed by an order-0 Markov chain that cannot represent a codon, and failed by HyenaDNA; the aperiodic control shows the test responds to position-modulo-three periodicity, not to frame. An entropy-steering direction transplanted to HyenaDNA is specific by a wide margin — −0.984 nats against +0.05 for every null — yet 0 of 250 steered cells lower entropy while accuracy holds, with accuracy falling to 0.257 against a chance rate of 0.25. It manufactures confidence without correctness. And a “protein-disjoint” split in my own sweep turned out to be disjoint in assay identifier, not in parent sequence: widening the panel admitted three independent beta-lactamase studies of one protein, putting the same sequence on both sides.

† That last one had been latent rather than wrong. Every reported run caps sequence length at 260 residues, which excludes a 286-residue protein by accident rather than by design. A label on a split is not a property of it.

Measurement gates

Eleven gates came out of closing those holes, each attributed to the experiment that paid for it. Gates 1 to 4 were violated by my own pipeline before they were written down. Three of those four share a single cause I did not see until they were on paper: they are all questions about the dose, and they only arise because the intervention is parameterised additively as h + αv. The standard causal test has no α at all.

01

Name which null and which readout. A matched-norm random direction is near-orthogonal to any fixed target in high dimension. The competing control is the generic mean shift.

05

Noise ceiling before null. A measured split-half ceiling of 0.71 to 0.85, flat across depth, so a negative result means the direction failed and not that the signal was absent.

08

Cluster, not row, as the statistical unit. The parent protein, not the assay — which is exactly what the beta-lactamase leak violated.

11

Run the test on a model that cannot hold the representation. A null sequence says what a model does on degraded input. A null model says whether the test needs the capability it is named for.

What is deliberately not claimed

Not that genomic language models fail to represent reading frame — only that this test cannot tell, so a verdict from it is evidence either way. Not that Markov chains are good models of coding sequence; that is a claim about the test. Absolute recovery never exceeds ten percent of the real difference, so the dominant fact about function in these representations is how little of it any extractable direction accounts for.

The title is broader than the causal evidence: two modalities are studied and only one is intervened in, so the depth findings should be read as protein findings until an intervention exists on the nucleotide side.

Status

An eight-page submission is drafted against the ICML main-track format, with the measurement ledger, the eleven gates and the full limitations list in appendices. Open work: a wider protein panel to pin the band's location, an interchange reformulation that removes the dose parameter entirely, a learned direction to replace difference-in-means, and a top-k attribution sweep to replace a best-of-39 single-atom scan.
