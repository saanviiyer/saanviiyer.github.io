gauntlet is an audit battery that stress-tests protein fitness proxies for trivial baselines and reward hacking. On measured PETase activity, a plain mutation count beat ESM-2 zero-shot.

It is part of the protein-lm-audits suite, and the shared question across that line of work is whether a proxy has earned the decision it is used for. Presented as a poster at the AI for Science workshop, NeurIPS 2026.
