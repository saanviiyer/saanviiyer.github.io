Sparse autoencoders, live

A sparse autoencoder pulls apart the tangled features a model packs into too few dimensions. I build one from scratch here and train it in your browser on a toy dataset where I know the ground-truth features. A Neuronpedia-style atlas at the end shows what each learned feature detects. Your browser computes everything on this page live, and I precomputed or made up nothing.

step 1 of 6

I use a toy with known ground truth because on a real model you never know the true features, so you cannot check whether the autoencoder found them. Here I pack 8 planted features into 6 dimensions and check whether the autoencoder recovers them. The superposition work this page builds on uses the same logic. The real Neuronpedia does this at scale on language models.
