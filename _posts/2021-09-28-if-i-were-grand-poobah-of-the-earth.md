*This started as a ninth-grade environmental science assignment: argue a sustainability plan as a mock campaign speech. It is one of my favorites, so I'm keeping it as it was.*

Fellow humans, thank you for considering me as a candidate for Exalted Grand Poobah of the Earth. I am humbled by the nomination and looking forward to the possibility of serving you. Allow me to introduce myself and my plans for bettering our planet.

I stand here as a passionate advocate for sustainability. If elected, I will do everything in my power to lower our ecological footprint without drastically compromising our quality of life. To do that, I plan to focus on two areas of impact: affluence and technology.

First, some grounding. An ecological footprint is the impact a person or community has on the environment, expressed in Earths. If everyone lived like that person, the footprint is the number of Earths it would take to sustain them. The world's current footprint is about 1.75 Earths, well past the one Earth we have. Ecologists capture this with a simple formula, I = PAT: total impact equals population times affluence (consumption per person) times technology (impact per unit of consumption).

My plan begins with technology, and specifically with plastic. Plastic packaging is enormously wasteful, and poor product design and weak infrastructure send most of it to landfills or into the environment, feeding global warming, greenhouse-gas emissions, and habitat destruction. I would aim to cut 40% of the plastic in common technologies within five years, starting by funding research into safe alternatives. Once a suitable substitute exists, I would work with carmakers first, reducing harmful plastic per vehicle from roughly 200 kg to 150 kg over two years, while keeping those cars fairly priced and helping distribute them to families who could not otherwise afford the switch. Alongside this, I would push nations and states to organize more plastic pickups and to teach young people about these issues so the next generation carries the movement forward.

The second part of my plan is a slower, long-term shift in affluence, through what we eat. Meat, in particular, carries a heavy environmental cost: greenhouse gases, deforestation, soil degradation, water stress, and coastal dead zones, plus a disproportionate share of agricultural land. Greenpeace calls industrial meat "the single biggest cause of deforestation, globally." So I would aim to cut global meat consumption by 50% over fifty years. Research in *The Lancet* suggests a target of no more than 300 g of meat per person per week by 2050; the average today is closer to 1,500 g. I would start with attainable milestones, easing toward 1,000 g per week over the next decade, so the change is gradual enough to live with. Some will object that this is unnecessary, but the ecological benefits outweigh the discomfort.

Our world needs change now more than ever. If elected Exalted Grand Poobah, I promise to do everything in my power to build a more sustainable world for our children. I'll leave you with a picture: a world where future generations thrive and look back on us with pride. Voting for me is the first step toward making it real. Thank you for listening.
