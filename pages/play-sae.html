Sparse autoencoders, live

A sparse autoencoder pulls apart the tangled features a model packs into too few dimensions. I train one from scratch in your browser on a toy model where I planted the true features myself, so you can watch it find them. The 3D view shows the activation space itself. Drag it to rotate. Your browser computes everything on this page, and I precomputed or made up nothing.
