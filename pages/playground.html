playground

it's ReLe that simple?

i built this to learn reinforcement learning by running it. twelve chapters follow stanford's cs 224r lecture by lecture, alongside aaditya nalawade's rl notes. the chapters start at a row of slot machines and end at rlhf, dpo and grpo. in between they cover value iteration, imitation, policy gradients, ppo, q-learning and offline rl. every agent learns live in your browser, and a test checks every number on the page.

livecode

inann

i built inann so you can walk through a trained network's insides in 3D, entirely in your browser. drop in your own ONNX model and its layers become rooms you can fly through. each layer gets a feature room, and you can steer the model or train a sparse autoencoder live on any layer.

livecode

sparse autoencoders, live

a step-by-step tutorial. i build a sparse autoencoder from scratch and train it in your browser on a toy dataset where i know the true features, so you can check whether it recovers them. a neuronpedia-style atlas then shows what each learned feature detects. turn the sparsity dial yourself and watch units go dead. nothing is precomputed.

interactive

lattice

i built an AI-native research workspace that keeps a paper library, pdf highlights, linked notes and a force-directed knowledge graph in one app. you can also hand your library to a model built from your own records. the live demo opens on a sample workspace.

livecode

dice

dice is the multitrack build of trackstar. you play a hand-gesture instrument and record your vocals, and a producer agent mixes your takes and explains each move. the agent produces what you sing and leaves the songwriting to you.

livecode

speech in ultrasound and in silico

i ran one speech experiment through two very different windows on the brain, a functional ultrasound recording of auditory cortex and meta's tribe v2 brain model, to see where they agree. i found an onset-shift fit that reverses the direction of the original pipeline. i also measured the shape of the cortical response myself and probed what the model counts as speech. every result here is interactive.

interactivecode

biopack

i built a forward-only workspace for protein structure and stability, limited to a small panel of benign proteins. the demo pairs a molecular viewer of the real panel structures with a forward estimate and its error bar. refusal-first gates decide when the tool declines to answer. the model outputs in the demo are synthetic placeholders.

interactivecode

rxiver

i built rxiver as an arxiv research workspace. it recommends papers and files them into folders, exports citations, and holds research chats grounded in the papers you save.
