Research record

Research

I work on AI for life sciences and neuroscience: world models of navigation, continual learning, and reinforcement learning for drug and protein design, with interpretability for neural network models of cognition. The throughline is representation, how a system encodes its world and whether that encoding is good enough to act on. A claim earns a control before I trust it.

I also work on AI safety and evaluation, mostly on whether a benchmark or a safeguard measures what its name claims. My biosecurity work sits in the same place.
