what makes a predictive model build a usable internal variable, and what makes it keep one?

Abstract

a world model is a model that predicts what it will observe next, and to do that it has to hold an internal state. two questions follow. first, when does that state become a variable you can actually read and steer, rather than a tangle. i train action-conditioned world models on a robot's range sensors with no access to its heading, and a faithful heading variable appears only under partial observability: a blackout-trained model tracks true heading at a fidelity of 0.99 against 0.58 for an always-on model, and the variable behaves like a continuous attractor and can be steered causally. second, what happens to an old skill when a model keeps training. i compare biologically inspired learning rules against backpropagation at matched learning, and once the comparison is fair the retention advantage of locality is under a third of a point, while replay adds about ten. both halves are about the same thing: an internal representation is only as good as the pressure that shaped it.

01 · The Approach

two studies, one idea about internal state

both studies ask what shapes the variable a model carries, so i keep the measurement honest in the same way each time. in the heading study i never give the model pose and i never put a pose term in the loss, so any heading variable has to emerge on its own, and i measure fidelity only at the steps where the model must integrate, deep inside a blackout with no observation. in the continual-learning study i never let a fancy rule compete against a weak baseline, so i match the learning accuracy of every method before i compare how much it forgets. in both, the control is the point: a ring that appears in every model is not evidence until fidelity separates the conditions, and a retention gain is not evidence until the baseline is tuned as hard as the rule.

02 · Results

two findings

a faithful heading variable emerges only under partial observability

RoBoWoMo @ IEEE IROS 2026Lightning talk and poster

i train action-conditioned world models on egocentric range observations from a robot moving in a plane, with no pose in the input and none in the loss, and ask whether a heading variable appears on its own. hidden states form a ring in nearly every condition, including control models that cannot integrate heading, so ring shape alone is not evidence. the real test is fidelity, how closely the coordinate around the ring follows physical heading at the steps where the model has to integrate without seeing anything. blackout training, where observations are withheld for stretches, produces a ring that tracks heading at a fidelity of 0.99, against 0.58 for an always-on model, so the faithful variable only appears when the task forces the model to hold it.

the faithful ring behaves like a continuous attractor, which is the structure neuroscience proposes for head-direction cells: a perturbation off the ring decays back to it, while a shift in position along the ring is held rather than erased. rotating the ring plane changes the model's predicted observation to the one for the rotated heading, so the variable is used causally and not just correlated with heading.

Finding 1

with no pose supervision, a predictive world model builds a heading variable that tracks true heading at a fidelity of 0.99 only under partial observability, against 0.58 when trained always-on, and the variable is a causally steerable continuous attractor.

a fair baseline erases the retention advantage of local learning rules

personal site

Blog

biologically inspired learning rules are often reported to forget less than backpropagation, so i tested whether the advantage survives a fair comparison on class-incremental Split-CIFAR-10. a rule that learns each new task less well also has less to forget, so i first calibrated an instrument: weakening backpropagation along a ladder of settings makes backward transfer almost a deterministic function of learning accuracy, with an R-squared of 0.995, which sets how small a difference i can resolve, a held-out error of 1.09 points. i then scored each rule against backpropagation at the same learning accuracy, tuning the baseline as hard as the rule. feedback alignment and direct feedback alignment were indistinguishable from backpropagation, predictive coding retained 0.21 points more with a 95% interval of [+0.11, +0.31], about a fifth of the instrument's own error, and replay added 10.6 points. so any retention advantage from locality on this protocol is under a third of a point.

i care about this beyond neuroscience because continued training of a deployed model, whether fine-tuning or reinforcement learning from human feedback, can erase capabilities and safety behaviors the model had before. that is catastrophic forgetting with a deployment address, and the one constraint engram adds is that any method claiming to protect earlier behavior has to be compared at matched learning of the new task.

Finding 2

once learning accuracy is matched and the baseline is tuned fairly, the retention advantage of local rules is under a third of a point while replay adds 10.6, so locality itself does not reduce forgetting on this protocol.

Takeaways

what both studies share

pressure builds the variable
a faithful heading variable appears at fidelity 0.99 only when a blackout forces the model to integrate.
it is a real attractor
off-ring perturbations decay, a phase shift along the ring persists, and rotation is causal.
a fair baseline is everything
matched learning erases the local-rule retention edge, leaving under a third of a point.
forgetting is an alignment problem
continued training erases old behavior the same way it erases an old task.

Limitations

the heading study is in simulation on a planar robot with range sensors, so whether the same faithful variable appears from richer sensing or on hardware is untested here. the continual-learning result is scoped to class-incremental Split-CIFAR-10 with a growing head, which sits near a retention floor, and a wider-window replication is the next step. the engram code is not public yet, so the Code link is held until it is released.
