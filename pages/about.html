About Me

✦   Hello! I'm Saanvi, an undergraduate at Caltech studying Computation and Neural Systems with minors in Information Science and Math, where I'm advised by Markus Meister. I'm part of Caltech's Integrated Core interdisciplinary honors program.

I build technologies to understand the brain — working at the intersection of neuroscience, AI, and computational biology. Across my research I keep returning to the same question: how do we turn messy, high-dimensional biological signals — brain MRIs, EEG, single-cell genomics, CT scans — into representations that are actually meaningful, and sometimes clinically useful?

Right now that looks like a few things at once. At Stanford Medicine, I'm building a multimodal foundation model that pairs pediatric brain MRIs with EEG to make epileptic lesion detection more sensitive. In the Perona Lab at Caltech, I'm developing a tri-modal radiogenomic model that aligns 3D kidney CT features with patients' gene-expression data using contrastive learning. And in the Shimojo Psychophysics Lab, I analyze EEG and imaging data from noninvasive brain-stimulation studies using focused ultrasound, EEG, and fMRI, while designing my own multisensory-integration experiments.

Before Caltech, I spent a summer at Seattle Children's Research Institute building machine-learning models to trace how medulloblastoma tumors form from developmental cell lineages — work I presented at Stanford's Comprehensive Cancer Research Training Program, where it won the People's Choice Award. Earlier still, I designed and built energy-harvesting circuits at the UW Sensor Systems Lab.

Outside of research, I care a lot about bringing more people into science. I founded and led a SWENext chapter, mentoring 50+ students through hackathons and STEM outreach, and today I'm the undergraduate head of Caltech's Neurotechers and Neuro/ML groups, where I'm organizing our first-ever Neurohackathon in partnership with the Allen Institute.

A few things I love outside the lab
  • Singing. I was a lead alto in my school's top jazz choir and a multi-year Washington All-State / All-Northwest performer, and I still sing whenever I can.
  • Building community. From running weekly speaker events and paper-reading groups to peer advocacy at Caltech, I like creating spaces where people can get excited about ideas together.
  • The outdoors. Hiking and getting out into the mountains is how I reset — the photo here is from one of those days.
