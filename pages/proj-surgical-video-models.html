--- layout: project title: "medical video models" permalink: /pages/projects/surgical-video-models/ ---

This is ongoing work on whether video foundation models use temporal information when they score medical movement, for RCP. Across the current encoders a single still frame matched the full 16-frame video, which is a preliminary result rather than a conclusion, and the next experiments isolate order, change, and static appearance so the representation claim can be falsified.
