experiences

a few places i have worked, and what each one meant to me.

Stanford Pediatric Neuroradiology Lab, Stanford Medicine

with Caltech SURF · summer 2026

  • Optimized an AI computer-vision model for brain-abnormality detection.
  • Built a web application now in trial with clinical radiologists at Stanford.
  • Post-training and evaluation for clinical EEG foundation models.

It was great to collaborate directly with clinicians and build AI software that fits into their workflow. It was also my first summer in SF, full of personal growth, and I came away inspired by the people there pushing the frontier of AI x bio.

Perona Laboratory, Caltech

computer vision · dec 2025 – present

  • Training a tri-modal radiogenomic model that aligns 3D kidney CT scans with gene-expression and mutation data.

Shimojo Psychophysics Laboratory, Caltech

oct 2025 – present

  • Analyzing EEG and imaging from noninvasive brain-stimulation studies using focused ultrasound and fMRI.

Pattwell Lab, Seattle Children's Research Institute

summer 2025

  • Built ML models on ~1M medulloblastoma cells to identify malignant populations; presented at the Stanford CCRTP.

University of Washington Sensor Systems Laboratory

2023 – 2024

  • Designed and tested novel energy-harvester topologies in an anechoic chamber.
