Computational research in living systems

Saanvi S. Subramanian.

I build models of living systems to make their behavior more understandable, and eventually more changeable.

I am a Caltech undergraduate studying Computation and Neural Systems. My work uses machine learning to study brains and other biological systems, with an emphasis on questions that can lead to useful interventions.

I am also curious about what biological intelligence can teach us about alignment, including neuro-based world models. Increasingly capable biological AI raises a second question: how should those systems be evaluated safely?

Current direction

Understanding internal representations in neural and biological systems well enough to support deliberate, evidence-based intervention.
