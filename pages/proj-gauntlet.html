gauntlet

gauntlet is an audit battery that stress-tests protein fitness proxies for trivial baselines and reward hacking. A plain mutation count beat ESM-2 zero-shot on measured PETase activity. The shared question across this work is whether a proxy has earned the decision it is used for. gauntlet is part of the protein-lm-audits suite, alongside shibboleth (biosecurity screening) and crosstalk (binding specificity).
