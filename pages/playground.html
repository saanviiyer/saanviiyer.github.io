playground

perceive

three browser labs on whether a world model keeps a stable picture of physical reality. a place-cell remapping audit for generated 3d rooms, my trained robowomo world models running live so you can black out a robot's sensors and watch its heading ring hold or drift, and a model of the fruit fly's head-direction circuit you can lesion pathway by pathway.

interactive

it's ReLe that simple?

learn reinforcement learning by running it. twelve chapters follow stanford's cs 224r lecture by lecture, alongside aaditya nalawade's rl notes. they go from a row of slot machines through value iteration, imitation, policy gradients, ppo, q-learning and offline rl, to rlhf, dpo and grpo. every agent learns live in the browser, and every number on the page is checked by a test.

livecode

inann

walk through a trained network's insides in 3D, entirely in the browser. drop in your own ONNX model and its layers become rooms you can fly through, with feature rooms, steering, and a sparse autoencoder trained live on any layer.

livecode

lattice

an AI-native research workspace. a paper library, pdf highlights, linked notes, and a force-directed knowledge graph in one app, with a way to hand your library to a model built from your own records. the live demo opens on a sample workspace to explore.

livecode

dice

a hand-gesture instrument and vocal studio with a producer agent, the multitrack build of trackstar. the agent mixes your takes and explains each move; it produces what you sing rather than writing the song for you.

livecode

speech in ultrasound and in silico

one speech experiment run through a functional ultrasound recording of auditory cortex and through meta's tribe v2 brain model. interactive results: the onset-shift fit that reverses the original pipeline, the measured response shape, and what the model treats as speech.

interactivecode

biopack

a forward-only workspace for protein structure and stability on a bounded panel of benign proteins. the demo pairs a molecular viewer over the real panel structures with a forward estimate and its error bar, and the refusal-first gates that decide when not to answer. model outputs in the demo are synthetic placeholders.

interactivecode

rxiver

an arxiv research workspace: it recommends papers, files them into folders, exports citations, and holds research chats grounded in the papers you save.
