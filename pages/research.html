Research

My work starts with noisy, incomplete measurements of living systems and asks how computation can recover the structure that matters. I currently work across neural signals, medical imaging, cellular data, and protein representations. The biological scale changes; the methodological problem does not.

Current research question

How can multimodal models make biology legible enough to support a trustworthy decision?

That means pairing measurements that resolve one another, testing what the learned representation actually contains, and being explicit about where a model stops being reliable. My long-term direction is adaptive neural interfaces; my present work builds and stress-tests the pieces such systems would depend on.
