research areas

multiscale biological and molecular modeling. the same biological event lives at several scales at once, from a single base change in DNA through a protein's shape to the tissue where it finally shows up on a scan, and a model earns its keep only if it respects more than one of them. the question i keep returning to is whether a model that reads one scale has captured the mechanism at another or only a correlate of it. in PRISM i pushed all 78 COSMIC mutational signatures through protein-coding DNA, embedded the wild-type and mutant proteins with ESM-2, and asked whether a mutational process steers proteins toward one protein family; SBS17a ranks first of 78 toward PF17041 across two independent clans, but a composition-matched control reproduces 72% of that movement and protein length explains 92.5% of it, so the clean cross-scale signal is mostly an embedding effect rather than a functional driver. CortSeer works the same seam from the clinic inward, joining a structural MRI detector to a functional localization prior so that restricting the search to the lesion lobe recovers 14 of 24 missed epileptogenic lesions against 1.4 expected by chance, reported as the ceiling a real EEG-derived prior would approach. where this is going is a protein-dynamics world model that carries a molecule forward in time instead of scoring a frozen sequence, so the representation can be tested for the mechanism directly rather than for a proxy of it. [TODO: Saanvi to confirm the protein-dynamics world-model scope and add a link.]

causal and mechanistic scientific reasoning. a model can report the right number for the wrong reason, so most of this work is separating a result that reflects a mechanism from one that reflects how it was measured. the question is whether a model's apparent competence survives a control that removes the shortcut, and most of what i build is the control rather than the model. in the action-identifiability work, across all 3,003 pairs of 78 mutational processes, distinct processes often land in the same place in a model's representation, with a translation placeholder count explaining up to an R-squared of 0.870 of the apparent distance, so a protein-design agent is often choosing between actions it cannot tell apart. the verifier work makes the same point for agent loops, where a scorer that looks near-perfect at an AUROC of 0.991 adds only 0.028 good candidates per batch over random, and the white-box evaluation i have proposed is the constructive version, training linear probes on a protein model's internal activations and holding whole families out so a model that only shortcuts an assay is caught when the probe fails to transfer. on the cognition side i fit disentangled recurrent networks to 487 participants in a bandit task space with Google DeepMind and the University of Edinburgh to recover the learning rule a person actually uses, which is ongoing and not yet reporting conclusions.

also interested in, as directions i want to build toward:

infrastructure for autonomous AI scientists. the shared audit harnesses, baselines, and verified nulls an autonomous scientist needs, so a loop can tell a real result from a plausible one before it acts on it.

sequential experimental design and real-world execution. policies that choose the next experiment and decide when to commit, wait, or defer, judged on real-world execution rather than on a static benchmark.

self-improving, closed-loop research systems. systems that read their own results and revise the next experiment, with the control discipline built in so the loop improves on signal rather than on its own artifacts.

gauntlet stress-tests a protein fitness proxy before anyone ranks designs with it; on 504 PET-hydrolase variants an ESM-2 650M zero-shot score ranks activity at a Spearman of 0.01 while a plain mutation count reaches 0.25. poster, AI for Science at NeurIPS 2026. code, full page.

faithful heading trains world models on a robot's range sensors with no pose supervision and finds a heading variable that tracks truth at 0.99 fidelity under blackout against 0.58 always-on, behaving as a causally steerable attractor. lightning talk and poster, RoBoWoMo at IEEE IROS 2026. biologos matches a genomic model and a protein model on 25 deep mutational scanning assays; Evo 2 7B reaches a mean Spearman of 0.266, indistinguishable from BLOSUM62 at 0.228 and far below ESM-2 650M at 0.466. paper, code, blog.

action identifiability asks whether a protein-design agent's wins identify the process behind them; across 3,003 pairs of 78 processes distinct ones share destinations, with a translation placeholder count explaining up to an R-squared of 0.870 in ESM-2 650M. poster, AgenticLS at NeurIPS 2026, and presented at MoML 2026, MIT. paper.

benchmark integrity scores a hazardous-knowledge safety benchmark without reading the question; a longest-answer rule hits 46.5% and a question-blind classifier 34.6% against 25% chance, so part of the score is an answer-writing artifact. blog.

engram tests whether biologically inspired local learning rules forget less than backpropagation at matched learning accuracy; the best retains 0.21 points more [+0.11, +0.31] while replay adds 10.6, so locality itself does not help. poster, AI4MetaScience at NeurIPS 2026. blog, code to be released.
