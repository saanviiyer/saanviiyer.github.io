steerable heading

Predictive world models recover a steerable heading variable with no pose supervision, but only under partial observability.

I ran ML simulations to understand how a robot perceives its environment from its range sensors, which connects to how brains build internal models of space from partial observations. A lightning talk and poster at the RoBoWoMo workshop, IEEE IROS 2026, and a poster at the Robotics World Modeling workshop at NeurIPS 2026.
