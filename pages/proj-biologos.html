biologos

biologos measures what transfers between biological sequence models, and what only looks like it does. DNA and protein are two encodings of the same molecule related by an exactly known map, which makes them a clean place to test whether a model trained on one representation genuinely knows something about the other, or whether apparent transfer is an artifact of how the question was asked.

Matched on the same deep mutational scanning assays, a protein model clears the bar, while the best genomic model's protein-fitness signal is statistically indistinguishable from a substitution matrix and is beaten by a handful of hand-picked chemical properties. Much of the repository is a corrections ledger: a reading-frame result retracted, a representation-transfer claim falsified by its own frameshift and reverse-complement controls, and a context effect that turned out to be composition.
