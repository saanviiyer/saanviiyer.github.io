Beyond Research

My music and side projects come from the same habit as my research. I chase an odd question and learn the most when a quick test breaks.

Music

Music is the thing I keep coming back to outside the lab. I sing (lead alto, three-time Washington All-State performer) and produce my own tracks in GarageBand. Choir trained a part of me that research uses every day. I listen closely, and I try to make my small part serve the whole.

Selected side projects

These are small prototypes, usually built over a weekend to test one idea, not to become a company.

Research tools

rxiver recommends and organizes arXiv papers, while Deadline Radar tracks ML, neuroscience, and computational-biology conference deadlines. Both came from friction in my own research workflow.

Human-in-the-loop automation

AuthzSwarm coordinates authorized defensive security checks. DraftDeck drafts social posts but never publishes them. Resume Tailor adapts application materials without inventing experience. Each one asks where an AI system should stop and hand control back to a person.

Creative computing

mixcut makes beat-aligned edits for DJ mixes, handsynth turns webcam hand-tracking into a browser instrument, and ConcertMatch connects listening habits to nearby live shows.

I have also built goal-planning, job-matching, and budgeting prototypes. The projects above best represent the questions I keep returning to.
