Beyond Research

Music

Music is the thing I keep coming back to outside the lab. I sing (lead alto, three-time Washington All-State performer) and produce my own tracks in GarageBand. Choir trained a part of me that research uses every day. I listen closely, and I try to make my small part serve the whole.
