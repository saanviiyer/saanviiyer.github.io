Medical AI and foundation models for living systems

Saanvi S. Subramanian.

I build models of living systems to make their behavior more understandable, and eventually more changeable.

I am a Caltech undergraduate studying Computation and Neural Systems. My work builds foundation models and representation-learning methods for medical imaging and biological data, with an emphasis on questions that can lead to useful clinical interventions.

I also work on AI safety and alignment, including what neuro-based world models can teach us about it and how increasingly capable AI in biology should be evaluated. That interest extends to biosecurity, and to federated, privacy-preserving learning for clinical data that cannot leave the institutions holding it.

Current direction

Building foundation models whose internal representations of neural and biological systems are trustworthy enough to support deliberate, evidence-based intervention.
