## Welcome!

I'm a Computation and Neural Systems undergraduate researcher at the **California Institute of Technology**. My research focuses on **brain stimulation and medical AI**, with interest in BCI development. I work across three active labs (Perona, Shimojo, Adolphs) and am a 2026 Caltech SURF fellow at Stanford Pediatric Radiology.

---

## Research Interests

- Brain stimulation and neural signal processing
- Multimodal medical foundation models
- Predicting mutation effects with protein language models
- Topic modeling in brain tumor development
- Multisensory perception in humans

---

## Experience

**Stanford Pediatric Neuroradiology** · Caltech SURF Fellow · *June 2026 –*  
Building a multimodal foundation model for pediatric brain MRIs with EEG for epileptic lesion detection. Using SimCLR, 3D ViT, and NLP to map EEG-identified seizure onset zones to anatomical regions.

**Perona Laboratory – Computer Vision Research** · *December 2025 – Present*  
Developing a tri-modal radiogenomic foundation model using contrastive learning to align 3D kidney CT scan features with gene expression mutation data. Processed 10,000+ paired CT-genomic samples; implementing vision transformers for multimodal medical embedding.

**Stanford Precision Neurotherapeutics Lab** · *June 2026 –*  
Analyzing neural responses to TMS using EEG recordings. Developing signal processing pipelines to reduce TMS-induced artifacts and extract meaningful neural signatures.

**Shimojo Psychophysics Laboratory** · *October 2025 – Present*  
Analyzing EEG and imaging data to evaluate noninvasive brain stimulation studies (focused ultrasound, EEG, fMRI). Leading and designing multisensory integration studies.

**Seattle Children's Research Institute – Pattwell Lab** · *Summer 2025*  
Built 9 regression and random forest models predicting cancerous cell formation with >90% accuracy from 1M medulloblastoma cells. Won People's Choice Award at Stanford Comprehensive Cancer Research Training Program.
