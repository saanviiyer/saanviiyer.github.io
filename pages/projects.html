personal projects

things i have built to chase a question, with a short note on why.

Faithful Heading in Predictive World Models

rl · world models · lightning talk + poster, RoBoWoMo @ IEEE IROS 2026

  • World models trained from pixels alone recover a faithful heading variable with no pose supervision.
  • The heading representation emerges only under partial observability, and transfers to unseen arenas.

PRISM

protein language models · oral, IEEE CIBCB 2026

  • A homology-free probe that applies mutational signatures to coding sequences and measures the shift in protein-language-model embeddings.

Engram

continual learning · neuroai

  • A controlled test of whether biologically inspired learning rules improve retention once training budget is matched.

Gauntlet

protein design · ai evaluation

  • An audit tool that stress-tests a fitness proxy for trivial-baseline dominance and reward-hacking before a wet lab trusts it.

plimsoll

ai safety · research tooling

  • A public command-line auditor that holds a research repo against methodology gates and reports the evidence behind each verdict.

CortSeer

medical imaging · oral, ECCV AI for Medical 3D Vision workshop

  • Maps EEG-identified seizure onset zones to anatomy on a multimodal MRI and EEG foundation model.
