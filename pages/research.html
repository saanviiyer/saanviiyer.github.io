Research

I use machine learning to understand the brain and other biological processes that matter in medicine. I am most interested in models that can support an intervention, not only make a prediction. A useful model should provide enough evidence to justify an action.

Neuro-based world models are a direction I want to pursue: I want to understand how brains represent their environment and use those representations to choose behavior. I am also beginning to study alignment and biosecurity as AI systems become more capable in biology.
