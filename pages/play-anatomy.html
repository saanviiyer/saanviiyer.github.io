Anatomy of a model

This is a working transformer with 8 dimensions, 2 attention heads and 6 words. Click an organ to read its live numbers. Switch one off to see what the model loses.

How to read this. I set every weight by hand so each part does one job you can watch, and I did not train the model. The first head reads position and looks one word back. The second head looks at the word itself. The second head looks at the word itself and writes back at a quarter of the strength. The feed-forward layer amplifies whatever the stream carries most loudly. The model therefore answers with the previous word, and whichever organ you switch off tells you which part carried that behaviour. Head 1 is the clearest cut, since the answer falls back to the word the model is standing on.
